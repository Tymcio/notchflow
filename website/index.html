FAQ

Which Macs does NotchFlow support?

Any Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 14 or later. On a MacBook the island wraps the notch; on a Mac mini, Mac Studio, iMac, or any display without a notch it sits as a small capsule in the middle of the menu bar. With several displays it follows the cursor, or stays on the display you choose.

Does NotchFlow collect my data?

The v1 app sends no telemetry. Network is used for update checks, license validation, optional lyrics, and — with the Agents add-on — quota lookups from those providers.

Can I build the app myself?

Yes. The source is GPL-3.0 on GitHub. A self-build runs the free tier. Premium and Agents still need a license key.

What is Premium, and what is Agents?

Premium is customization, lyrics, the camera mirror, notifications, a larger shelf, and higher note and clipboard limits. Agents is a separate €14.90 add-on for coding tools in the notch. See the comparison.
