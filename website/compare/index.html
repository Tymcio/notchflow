When NotchFlow is the right pick

You use Claude Code, Codex or Cursor every day and also want music, a file shelf and timers at the top of the screen, in one native app instead of three. You want to read the source, keep clipboard history off unless you turn it on, and pay once.

When something else is better

If you only want agent approvals, a free agent-only app such as coucou is enough. If you only want usage limits for many providers in the menu bar, CodexBar covers more of them. If you want the most polished all-in-one notch app and don't need open source, NotchNook is the established choice.
